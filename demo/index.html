Photosensitivity warning

At its default Exposure this effect only flashes where the clip does. Turned up, Exposure turns a clip's own pulses up too, so a pulsing video can flash brightly through it: at +2 stops 3 of Resolume's 33 demo clips flash faster than three times a second through it, where the clips on their own did not. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. This page's generated clips move slowly and don't pulse; a video of your own can.
